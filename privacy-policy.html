Mindfulness by Shiva · Last updated 24 September 2026

This policy explains what the Mindfulness by Shiva app ("the app") collects, why, and what you can do about it. The app is published by Ranjith K ("we"). Contact: victoran42k@gmail.com.

That is everything that leaves your phone. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.

What stays on your phone

Your practice history, favourites, reading progress, sound settings and morning-notification time are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to us. Morning notifications are scheduled locally on the phone. Spoken instructions use your device's built-in text-to-speech engine.

Service providers

Sign-in is handled by Google (Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication), which processes the data above on our behalf under Firebase's privacy and security terms. Data may be processed on Google servers outside your country.

How long we keep it

Your account data is kept while you have an account. When you delete your account it is removed from Firebase Authentication right away. Google may keep security logs for a limited period under its own policies.

Deleting your account

In the app, tap your profile picture, then Delete account. This deletes your account and clears the practice data stored on the phone. If you can no longer open the app, email victoran42k@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and we will delete it within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in India), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data-protection authority. To use any of these rights, write to victoran42k@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or under the minimum age for digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Health

The app offers attention and meditation exercises. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical or psychological advice or treatment.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app.
